# Phishing Awareness & Security Training

Internal security awareness training deck built for the **CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship** (Task 2). The training teaches employees to recognize phishing emails, spot fraudulent websites, understand common social engineering tactics, and respond correctly when targeted — reinforced with two real-world-style case studies and a 3-question interactive knowledge check.

## Overview

Phishing remains one of the leading causes of corporate data breaches. This training was designed to move beyond generic warnings and give employees concrete, visual pattern recognition — what a phishing email actually looks like, what a spoofed login page actually looks like, and which psychological tactics attackers rely on.

## Contents

| Section | Covers |
|---|---|
| **I. Understanding the Threat** | What phishing is, why it works, and its business impact |
| **II. Recognizing an Attack** | Anatomy of a phishing email, identifying fraudulent websites, social engineering tactics (urgency, authority, fear, curiosity/reward) |
| **III. Case Studies in Practice** | CEO fraud / Business Email Compromise (BEC), and a smishing (SMS phishing) delivery scam |
| **IV. Defense & Assessment** | Best practices checklist, a 3-question scenario-based knowledge check, and an answer key with rationale |

## File

## Key Concepts Covered

- Identifying spoofed sender domains, urgency-based subject lines, and generic greetings
- Verifying a website before entering credentials (URL inspection, HTTPS/padlock caveats, layout red flags)
- The four social engineering levers attackers use: **urgency, authority, fear, curiosity/reward**
- How Business Email Compromise (CEO fraud) and smishing scams are actually executed
- Practical, low-friction defenses: verifying senders, hovering links, MFA, out-of-band confirmation, and reporting rather than deleting
